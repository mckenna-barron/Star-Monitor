ATTIS Mars Mission - Recommended Health Interventions

  • Behavioral Health Interventions:

  • If you are feeling anxious or depressed, try TIPP!

  • T: Temperature

    Hold something cold/ hot (not enough to cause damage to you)

    I: Intense Exercise

    If you can, use this time to exercise in some way to get your heart rate up

    P: Paced Breathing

    Try box breathing. 4 seconds in, 4 seconds hold, 4 seconds out, 4 seconds hold. Repeat until the panic goes away.

    P: Progressive Muscle Relaxation

    Tense a specific muscle group (ex. legs) as much as possible for 5 seconds, then release. Repeat with another muscle group.

  • Write it down.

    In a journal or on a digital device, write down your feelings.
