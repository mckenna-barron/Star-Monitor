  • How to Assess Bilirubin

    Use the urine collection kit to void, then draw a syringe of urine. Wearing protective gloves, apply the urine

    in small amounts to each reagent strip on the urine dipstick. Then, blot away excess. Wait 2 minutes for the

    reaction to happen. If bilirubin is present, the reagent strip should turn a dark purple, red, or brown. This

    would mean it is positive. If there is no color change, mark it as negative.
