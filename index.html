Welcome to Star Monitor!

  • Welcome to NASA Space Apps StarMonitor! StarMonitor is a health monitoring app which records data for real time health recommendations and trend information. Simply input

    your health information on the Input Health Data page, then view recommendations based off your data. If looking for trends, view them in History. Every day you input your

    health data, Mars the Cat gets one step closer to home! To meet Mars, log in. To navigate back to the home page, click on the Space Apps logo.

  • This website was designed using VS Code for the NASA Space Apps Challenge from 11/14/2026-11/15/2026
